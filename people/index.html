FACULTY PROFILE

Department of Transportation Planning and Management, Tongji University

Jian Li is an Associate Professor and PhD supervisor at the School of Transportation, Tongji University. His research and practice focus on transportation management under non-routine conditions, emergency decision support, and urban transportation planning and policy. In recent years, he has focused on applying large language models and AI agents to urban transportation governance. He has led projects funded by the National Key R&D Program of China, the National Natural Science Foundation of China, and Sino-German international cooperation programs. As first or corresponding author, he has published over 50 academic papers. His outputs include one academic monograph, 11 granted invention patents, five software copyrights, and one group standard. His research has received first prizes in the Huaxia Construction Science and Technology Awards and the Urban Planning Society of China Science and Technology Progress Awards, and second prizes in the Natural Resources Science and Technology Awards and the China Highway & Transportation Society Science and Technology Progress Awards. He has supervised students who won first prizes in the big data track of the Digital China Innovation Contest and the Chengyuan Cup Planning Decision Support Model Design Competition.
