Collaborative supervision of dangerous goods supply chain: A blockchain-based conceptual platform

王奥, 朱国军, 李健
二月 2025
源文档 DOI
Blockchain-based supervision platform architecture. Source: paper.

摘要

Effective supervision of dangerous-goods supply chains requires collaboration among multiple agencies and reliable protection against concealment and false reporting. This study develops a blockchain-based conceptual platform with blockchain, storage, interface, and interaction layers. Three IPFS and FISCO BCOS data-integration modes support heterogeneous data sharing, while a smart-contract-based traceable state machine records the supervision process and clarifies accountability.

类型
Journal Publications
出版物
Computers & Industrial Engineering, 200, 110818

研究概览

本研究面向危险货物供应链中的多部门协同监管、数据孤岛与责任追溯问题，设计了由区块链层、存储层、接口层和交互层组成的概念平台。平台结合 FISCO BCOS、IPFS 和智能合约，支持多类数据的链上共享与监管过程追踪。

核心方法与发现

  • 根据数据体量与隐私需求，设计三种数据集成和链上共享模式。
  • 将可追溯状态机编码为智能合约，保留监管任务流转和责任链条。
  • 八节点原型实验表明，方案能够在可接受的延迟和吞吐水平下支撑数据共享与复杂监管追溯。
